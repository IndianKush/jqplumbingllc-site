Common questions

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free within San Antonio city limits.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. JQ Plumbing is fully licensed and insured. Our Texas plumbing license is RMP-47009, and our plumbers are fingerprint background checked.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Weekends are for scheduled and emergency work. We are not a 24 hour service.

What warranty do you offer?

All parts carry the manufacturer warranty. Our workmanship is covered for 90 days to 1 year, depending on the size and scope of the job.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes. Military, first responders and teachers get a discount with a valid ID.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We are a residential and commercial plumbing company.

What areas do you serve?

San Antonio and the surrounding areas, including Schertz, New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, Bandera, Converse, Kirby, City Base, Castroville, Lytle, Devine, Somerset, Elmendorf, Floresville, Bulverde, Stone Oak, Boerne, Marion, Seguin and Universal City.
